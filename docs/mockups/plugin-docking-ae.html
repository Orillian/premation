Plugin panels, docked the After Effects way

Proposal for review, not built yet. The goal is that panels and plugins work like After Effects: every panel, including Plugins and each plugin's own panel, is a normal tab. You can group it with other tabs, dock it beside another group to make a new column, or float it in its own window. Yellow numbers point to the notes under each frame.

1A plugin's panel is a normal tab. Deep Glow's own page sits in a group like any other tab, with the same tab grammar: × on the front tab, its name, and the ≡ panel menu. It is grouped with Plugins here because the user dragged it there.
2Grippers. Dragging the dotted grip on a tab moves that panel. The grip at the far right of the tab strip moves the whole group. A thin blue frame marks the active group, as in AE.
3Effects & Presets lists built-in effects only. Plugin effects appear in the Plugins panel and in search results. The right column keeps AE's stacked layout, with headers that collapse on click.
Columns are just docking. The plugin column above is not a special sidebar. It was made by dropping a panel on the left docking zone of the right column (frame 2). Any panel can make a column this way, on either side.

Five zones on every group. Four trapezoids along the edges dock the panel beside the group: left or right makes a new column, top or bottom splits the column. The rectangle in the middle, and the tab strip, put it into the group as a tab.

Only the zone under the cursor lights up, in AE's violet. The others are faint outlines, so you can see what the other choices are.

Window edges. The far left and right edges of the window get the same strip zone, so a panel can make a full-height column at the window edge.

Ctrl-drag, or dragging outside the window, floats the panel in its own window. Esc cancels a drag.

The ≡ panel menu carries AE's own items: Undock, Close, Maximize Frame (` key), and Panel Group Settings (Stacked or Tabbed). Below them come the panel's own items.

Window ▸ Extensions lists every installed plugin's panel, as AE does for extension panels. A check means it is open. Choosing one brings it to the front, or opens it where it was last docked.

The Window menu lists panels A–Z with their shortcuts, and Plugins is one of them.

One row per plugin, twirled open to show what it adds:

  • Effects: double-click applies to the selected layers, or drag onto a layer or the viewer, as in Effects & Presets.
  • Presets: the same gestures.
  • Panels: double-click opens them, and blue means already open.

Status is a word on the right (Loaded, Disabled, Update 1.3, Failed), not a button. Right-click a plugin for Enable or Disable, Update, Uninstall, and Show in Folder. Nothing on the row is a large button.

Browse Store opens the store page. Install there, and the plugin appears here with no restart.

Plugins is closed by default, like AE's extra panels. It opens the first time a plugin is installed, and from Window ▸ Plugins.
